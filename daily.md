# Maine Roundup — August 9, 2026

### 🔫 2nd District race turns into a gunfight

The battle to flip Maine's 2nd Congressional District is heating up, with both candidates claiming to be the true champion of gun rights. Former Governor Paul LePage scored a key endorsement from the NRA this week, which his campaign is using to hammer Democratic opponent Matt Dunlap over donations from groups that support stricter gun laws . Dunlap, a former executive director of the Sportsman's Alliance of Maine, has been touting his hunting background and even challenged LePage to a skeet shooting contest . The nonpartisan Cook Political Report now rates the district "Likely Republican" .

### ⚖️ Collins faces heat from both sides as Jackson rises

Senator Susan Collins is running against Democrat Troy Jackson, a fifth-generation logger from Allagash who entered politics after a protest against Canadian logging trucks . Collins announced she'll vote against confirming Todd Blanche as Attorney General, but a right-wing radio host called it a "savvy" move to help her reelection . Meanwhile, Maine Democrats are on a "Retire Susan Recess" tour, slamming Collins for supporting a procedural vote that moved Trump's budget bill forward—a vote they say cost over 30,000 Mainers their health insurance . Women voters, who make up the majority of Maine's electorate, are expected to be decisive in this race .

### 🗳️ What to watch

The NRA endorsement could prove crucial in a district where nearly 50% of households own a gun, but Democrats believe the 2023 Lewiston shooting has shifted voters toward favoring some firearm restrictions . Collins's strategy of breaking with Trump on Blanche may be tested by her broader record; a letter in the Press Herald calls on voters to retire her for voting to confirm Kavanaugh and Gorsuch . Jackson's ability to appeal to women voters, despite past reports of a short temper, will be key in a state where independent women often decide statewide races .
