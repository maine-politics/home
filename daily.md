# Maine Roundup — August 19, 2026

🗳️ **LePage opens 7-point lead in 2nd District**

Former Governor Paul LePage has opened a 48%-41% lead over Matt Dunlap, according to the UNH Survey Center. The Cook Political Report moved the race to "Lean Republican." Dunlap is struggling with turnout in Aroostook and Washington counties. [Full story →](index.html?id=lepage-2nd-district-lead)

⚖️ **Collins-Jackson fight over logging industry**

Susan Collins is running digital ads accusing Troy Jackson of "selling out" Maine loggers. Jackson called the ads a "desperate lie" and picked up the Maine AFL-CIO endorsement. The logging industry supports roughly 15,000 jobs statewide. [Full story →](index.html?id=collins-jackson-logging)

🏛️ **Lewiston memorial bill clears committee**

LD 2041, sponsored by Sen. Peggy Rotundo (D-Lewiston), passed unanimously out of the Veterans and Legal Affairs Committee. The bill allocates $500,000 for a permanent memorial and mental health resource center. Full Legislature votes August 26. [Full story →](index.html?id=lewiston-memorial-bill)

📊 **Voter registration: GOP gaining ground**

Republicans added 3,891 voters since May, compared to 2,104 for Democrats. Independents remain the largest bloc with 398,572 registered voters. [Full story →](index.html?id=voter-registration-august-2026)

🏛️ **Eastport pauses data center development**

The Eastport City Council enacted a moratorium on data center development, citing concerns about power consumption and environmental impact. [Full story →](index.html?id=eastport-data-center-pause)
