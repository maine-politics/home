Democrats Are Using Dead Mainers to Push Gun Control — And They Know It

By Matthew Hogan · August 19, 2026

The families of Lewiston are still grieving. Eighteen people were murdered in the deadliest mass shooting in Maine history. Thirteen more were wounded. The scars on that community will never fully heal.

And Democrats in Augusta just saw an opportunity.

On Tuesday, the Joint Committee on Veterans and Legal Affairs voted 13-0 to advance LD 2041, the bill to build a permanent memorial at the site of the former Schemengees Bar and Grille and Just-In-Time Recreation. Every Republican on the committee supported it. Every Democrat supported it. It was, by all appearances, a moment of genuine bipartisanship.

But don't be fooled. Behind the memorial photo-ops and the tearful press conferences, the Democratic machine is running the same playbook it always runs: use tragedy to push gun control.

The Memorial Is Real. The Bipartisanship Is Not.

The Democrats want you to believe that supporting a memorial is the same as supporting their broader agenda. It isn't. A memorial honors the victims. Gun restrictions punish the innocent.

The independent commission's final report was clear: law enforcement had "sufficient probable cause" to take Robert Card into protective custody weeks before the shooting. He was known to be in crisis. He had been hospitalized for mental health issues. He had made threats. He had access to weapons he should never have been able to possess.

And yet, he slipped through every crack in the system. Not because the laws were too weak — because the system failed to enforce the laws already on the books.

But Democrats don't want to talk about enforcement failures. They don't want to talk about the mental health crisis that allowed a disturbed man to fall through the cracks. They don't want to talk about the U.S. Army's failure to follow its own protocols, or the local sheriff's office that failed to act on clear warning signs.

They want to talk about guns. Because guns are easy. Guns are the boogeyman. Guns let them raise money and fire up their base without actually solving anything.

The Memorial Is a Distraction

Here's the truth they won't say out loud: the memorial bill, LD 2041, is the least controversial thing the Legislature will vote on this year. Of course it passed unanimously. Who's going to vote against a memorial for murdered Mainers?

But while the cameras were rolling on the memorial vote, the same Democrats were quietly pushing forward on their next round of gun restrictions. Expanded background checks. Magazine capacity limits. Waiting periods. The kind of laws that would do nothing to stop the next Robert Card — because Robert Card passed a background check. He used legally purchased firearms.

The problem was never the guns. The problem was a system that refused to connect the dots on a man in obvious crisis.

Democrats Are Exploiting the Dead

The worst part is that they know it. They know that every time they invoke Lewiston, they're using the memory of eighteen dead Mainers to advance an agenda that the victims' own families don't universally support. They know that "common sense gun safety" is just a euphemism for "more restrictions on law-abiding citizens."

And they know that Republicans will be painted as heartless if they object.

It's the oldest trick in the political playbook: hide behind the dead to avoid an honest debate.

What Actually Honors Lewiston

If Democrats truly wanted to honor the victims, they would focus on the real reforms that might have actually prevented the tragedy:

  • Mental health investment — so the next person in crisis gets help before they hurt anyone
  • Law enforcement accountability — so the next time a sheriff's office gets a warning about a dangerous individual, they act on it
  • Fixing the enforcement gaps — so that people who are already prohibited from owning firearms can't get them

But those aren't easy. Those don't fit on a campaign mailer. Those don't drive donations from anti-gun groups.

So instead, they'll pass a memorial, shed some tears for the cameras, and then get back to their real work: disarming Mainers.

The Bottom Line

Republicans should vote yes on LD 2041. The memorial is a good and decent thing. The victims deserve to be remembered.

But let's not pretend this changes anything about the gun debate. Democrats will still push their restrictions. They'll still use Lewiston as a cudgel. They'll still claim that anyone who opposes their agenda is "politicizing tragedy."

The truth is the opposite. It's the Democrats who are politicizing tragedy. They've been doing it since the bodies were still warm.

Mainers see through it. They know the difference between honoring the dead and exploiting them. And in November, they'll remember who stood for real solutions — and who just stood at the podium with a candle and an agenda.

Matthew Hogan is a political commentator covering Maine politics.
