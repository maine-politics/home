About Maine Politics

Maine Politics is an independent, no-paywall outlet covering Maine state and local government. It is reported and built from Bucksport, Maine.

What we cover

Straight news reporting on the Governor's office, the Maine Legislature, local and county government, and Maine elections — sourced directly from primary records: bill text and votes from the Maine Legislature's bill tracker, official releases from the Governor's office, and disclosures filed with Maine campaign finance regulators.

Opinion vs. news

Opinion pieces are clearly labeled and kept separate from news reporting. They reflect the views of the author, not a newsroom consensus, and are never presented as straight reporting.

Funding

This site carries no advertising, no paywall, and no outside funding. It is an independent, self-published project.

Corrections

If you find an error in a story, please get in touch and it will be corrected promptly, with a note on the correction.
